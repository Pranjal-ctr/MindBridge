# Kio Brand Guidelines

> **Version:** 0.1 (MVP)
>
> **Status:** Active for MVP & Pilot Program

# Brand Overview

## Brand Name

**Kio**

## Brand Mission

Kio exists to help every student grow with confidence by providing a trusted AI companion that supports students, parents, counselors, and schools.

Kio is designed to become more than an application—it is a companion that understands, guides, and grows alongside every learner.

---

# Brand Vision

To become the world's most trusted AI companion for education, emotional wellbeing, and personal growth.

---

# Brand Values

## 🤝 Support

Every interaction should make users feel supported rather than judged.

---

## ❤️ Care

Empathy comes before intelligence.

Technology should always feel human.

---

## 🧠 Intelligence

Kio is smart, but never overwhelming.

It explains.
It guides.
It simplifies.

---

## 🌱 Growth

Every student is capable of growth.

Kio focuses on progress rather than perfection.

---

## 🔒 Trust

Privacy, transparency and safety are fundamental.

Trust is our greatest product.

# Tone of Voice

Always write as if speaking to a real person.

## Friendly

❌ Greetings, User.

✅ Hi! How are you feeling today?

---

## Encouraging

❌ You performed poorly.

✅ Let's work together and improve.

---

## Positive

❌ Wrong answer.

✅ Almost there! Let's try another way.

---

## Calm

Never create panic.

Even when discussing serious situations, communicate with clarity and reassurance.

# Logo Philosophy

The logo represents a friendly AI companion.

The smile underneath the wordmark symbolizes multiple ideas simultaneously:

* Support
* Encouragement
* Positivity
* Safety
* Growth

The smile is intentionally subtle.

It should never dominate the wordmark.

---

# Logo Usage

## Primary Logo

Navy wordmark with teal smile.

Use on:

* Website
* Mobile App
* Presentations
* Social Media
* Documents

---

## Light Version

For white backgrounds.

---

## Dark Version

For dark UI.

---

## Monochrome

Use only when color reproduction is not possible.

---

# Logo Rules

Do:

* Maintain clear space around the logo.
* Keep proportions unchanged.
* Use official brand colors.
* Scale proportionally.

Don't:

* Stretch the logo.
* Rotate the logo.
* Change colors.
* Add effects.
* Add shadows.
* Modify typography.
* Place on busy backgrounds.

# Icon Style

Icons should be:

* Rounded
* Minimal
* Two-pixel stroke (or equivalent)
* Consistent
* Friendly

Avoid:

* Sharp corners
* Complex illustrations
* Skeuomorphic styles

---

# Illustration Style

Soft.

Modern.

Human.

Illustrations should communicate:

* Learning
* Growth
* Curiosity
* Support
* Diversity
* Inclusion

Avoid overly cartoonish artwork.

---

# UI Principles

## Simplicity

Reduce cognitive load.

---

## Accessibility

Readable typography.

Good contrast.

Keyboard-friendly interactions.

---

## Calm Interface

Avoid excessive colors and animations.

Users should feel relaxed while using Kio.

---

## Human First

AI should always feel like an assistant—not the center of attention.

---

# Experience Principles

Every feature should answer at least one of these questions:

* Does it help the student?
* Does it reduce teacher workload?
* Does it help parents understand?
* Does it improve wellbeing?
* Does it build trust?

If not, reconsider the feature.
